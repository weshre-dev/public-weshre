WeShre General Space

A dedicated space to share your events to the rest of the world or find events organized by other users. We are already 11.500 in Belgium!

Your Customized Space

Better connect with your community in real life by customizing your community space to share and find events with fellow members. You have full control of all aspects.

Control all aspects of your community as if it was your own app!

Manage access and authorisation

Define how users can join your community : Open access, ask to join, pay a membership or connect to your user database. You can create various role with different authorisations to attribute to your members.

Define your design

Your community in WeShre is like having your own platform. Choose the colors, add your logo to offer your members an immersive experience

Manage who can post

Control who can publish events, publish news or create chats and groups in your community

Create sub-communities and groups

With WeShre, you have the power to build vibrant communities and organize them however you see fit. Create your own community, then easily set up sub-communities and groups within it to bring together people with shared interests, goals, or passions. Whether you're looking to foster deep discussions, collaborate on projects, or just hang out, WeShre’s flexible structure makes it simple to tailor your space. With the ability to create and organize everything in one place, WeShre helps you unlock endless possibilities for connection, collaboration, and growth, all while building a truly personalized experience.

One magic button to give you access to all the community’s events

Never miss an event with WeShre’s community calendar! Access it easily through the WeShre button in the menu and stay up-to-date with all the exciting events, meetups, and activities happening in your community. Whether it’s a workshop, hangout, or special gathering, the calendar keeps you in the loop so you can join and connect whenever you want. Simply click the WeShre button and dive into your next adventure!
